# gigs: Assess Fetal, Newborn, and Child Growth with International Standards

Convert between anthropometric measures and z-scores/centiles using WHO
and INTERGROWTH-21^(st) growth standards. This includes functions for
classification of newborn and infant growth, including size for
gestational age, stunting, wasting, and weight-for-age.
